# SpendWise (Android)

An offline, privacy-first personal finance & expense tracker for Android built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**.

## Features
- **Monthly Dashboard**: Track Net Balance, Income (salary, freelance, bonus), and Expenses (food, bills, rent, groceries, etc.) by month, with category spending breakdowns.
- **Transactions**: Searchable and filterable transaction history. Pay expenses via **Cash/Bank** or a **Credit Card** (automatically updates your card balance).
- **Budgets & Categories**: Set monthly spending limits per category with color-coded progress bars, plus create custom expense or income categories.
- **Wallet**:
  - **Credit Cards**: Track credit limits, current balances, utilization, due dates, and pay card bills.
  - **Savings**: Track savings goals (Emergency Fund, ASB, Travel, etc.) with quick Deposit/Withdraw buttons.
  - **Loans**: Track car/housing/study loans, monthly payments, payoff progress, and record repayments.
- **Settings**: Default currency **MYR (RM)** with support for USD, SGD, EUR, GBP, IDR, and PHP.

## Installing on Your Android Phone from GitHub
1. Push this repository to GitHub.
2. Go to the **Actions** tab and wait ~2–3 minutes for **Build Android APK** to turn green.
3. Open the **Releases** section on the right side of your GitHub repository.
4. On your Android phone, open that Release page, tap **`SpendWise.apk`** to download it, and tap to install.
